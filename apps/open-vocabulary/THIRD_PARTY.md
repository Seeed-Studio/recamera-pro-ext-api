# Third-party materials

- The YOLO-World V2.1-S detector and its CLIP text encoder are exported from
  the same [stage-1 checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/wondervictor/YOLO-World-V2.1/tree/c620164ee3979bf49b895c8a8e0f49aeaca89209).
  See the [pinned YOLO-World source](https://github.com/AILab-CVC/YOLO-World/tree/b1b09f2f0340ca7dede69e10b7e909c469677fd9)
  and its [GPL-3.0 license](licenses/YOLO-World.txt).
- The bundled vocabulary and BPE merges come from the pinned
  [CLIP tokenizer](https://huggingface.co/openai/clip-vit-base-patch32/tree/3d74acf9a28c67741b2f4f2ea7635f0aaf6f0268).
  See [OpenAI CLIP](https://github.com/openai/CLIP) and its [MIT license](licenses/CLIP.txt).
- The original ftfy and wcwidth wheels retain their license metadata.
- The package icon was generated using the SDK's [YOLO detector icon](../yolo-detector/icon.png) as a style reference.

[SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) records the upstream revisions, asset digests, and RKNN conversion settings.
